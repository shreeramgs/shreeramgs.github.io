# Biography

I am a software engineer at [White Hack Labs](https://whitehacklabs.com/) in New York, building AI-driven security tools for [HackerGPT](https://hackergpt.app/)—a platform serving **10,000+ users**. I completed my **Master's in Data Science** from the [University at Buffalo](https://www.buffalo.edu/) and worked as a Research Assistant at the [Center for Unified Biometrics and Sensors (CUBS) Lab](https://www.buffalo.edu/cubs.html) under Prof. Srirangaraj Setlur.

I'm actively seeking **PhD opportunities** in Computer Vision, focusing on **cross-modal world models** that learn physical properties through prediction discrepancy—the insight that when AI predicts cross-modal behavior and observes mismatch, the error reveals physical properties neither modality encodes alone.

## Research Vision

I propose developing **cross-modal world models** that learn physical properties through prediction discrepancy. The core insight from my GestSpoof work: when a system predicts the deformation that should accompany applied pressure and observes a mismatch, that error reveals physical properties—elasticity, hardness, material authenticity—that neither modality encodes alone.
